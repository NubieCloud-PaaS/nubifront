## Update your profile

From **Profile**, you can change:

- your **first name** (min. 2 characters),
- your **last name** (min. 2 characters),
- your **email**.

## Change your password

Changing your password goes through a **link sent by email** (the same mechanism as [Forgot password](/en/docs/compte#forgot-password)): click **Change password**, receive the link, set the new password.

## Delete your account

Deletion lives in the **danger zone** of your profile. The journey has several steps:

1. Nubiecloud checks whether you own any **resources** (projects, environments, applications).
2. If so, a **transfer preview** explains that those resources will be **transferred to the organization's owner**.
3. To confirm, you type the confirmation word shown on screen (**SUPPRIMER**) and tick the confirmation box.

> ⚠️ Deleting your account is **permanent**. Your resources are not destroyed: they are **transferred to the owner of the relevant organization**.
