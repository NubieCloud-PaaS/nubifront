Nubiecloud offers two complementary mechanisms to protect your account: **two-factor authentication (2FA)** and **device verification**.

## Two-factor authentication (2FA / TOTP)

2FA adds a temporary code on top of your password, generated by an authenticator app (Google Authenticator, Authy, Microsoft Authenticator…).

### Enabling 2FA

1. From your **profile**, choose **Enable two-factor authentication**.
2. Nubiecloud shows a **QR code** (plus a key you can type in manually). Scan it with your authenticator app.
3. Enter the **6-digit code** generated by the app to confirm.
4. **Save the 10 backup codes** that are displayed: they let you sign in if you lose your phone. They are shown **only once**.

### At sign-in

Once 2FA is active, after your password Nubiecloud asks for the **code from your app** (or a **backup code**). Each backup code can only be used once.

### Disabling 2FA

From your profile, disable 2FA by confirming with your **password** and a valid **code**.

> 🔒 Keep your backup codes somewhere safe. If you lose both your phone **and** your codes, you will need to contact support.

## Trusted devices

When you sign in from a **new device**, Nubiecloud may send you a **6-digit code by email** to confirm it really is you.

| Detail | Value |
|--------|--------|
| Trust duration | **30 days** |
| Email code validity | 10 minutes |
| Resending the code | up to 5 times per hour |

- Tick **Trust this device** so you are not asked again for 30 days in this browser.
- From your profile → **Trusted devices**, you can **list** your devices (browser, last activity, expiry), **revoke** one, or **revoke all the others** (useful if you suspect a device has been compromised).
