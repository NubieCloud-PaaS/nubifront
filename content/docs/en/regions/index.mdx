A **region** determines **where** your applications run. Nubiecloud distinguishes two kinds of regions.

| Type | Description |
|------|-------------|
| **Nubiecloud (global) region** | Infrastructure managed by Nubiecloud, shared across all organizations (e.g. **Europe**). |
| **Custom region** | **Your own infrastructure** (a VM/server you connect), private to your organization. |

> ℹ️ Additional regions (Africa) are announced in the console as **"coming soon"**: they appear in the selector but are not enabled yet.

## Choosing a region

The region is chosen when **creating a project** (**Region** field). Every application of the project runs in the chosen region.

- **Global regions** are available immediately.
- A **custom region** can only be selected once it is **connected** (see below).

## Connecting your own infrastructure

You can attach your own server as a region: Nubiecloud installs everything needed on it and links it securely. This is the **Connect** feature.

➡️ Full guide: **[Connecting my infrastructure](/en/docs/regions/connecter-mon-infra)**.
