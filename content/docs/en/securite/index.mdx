Nubiecloud applies several layers of security to protect your applications and your data.

## In short

| Area | What Nubiecloud does |
|---------|------------------------|
| **Isolation** | each environment runs in a **partitioned space** (namespace); each project has its own dedicated tenant |
| **Roles** | access managed by roles at the [organization](/en/docs/organisations/membres) (Owner/Admin/Member) and [project](/en/docs/projets) (Admin/Developer/User) level |
| **Secrets** | sensitive variables are **encrypted** and never shown again — see [Secrets](/en/docs/securite/secrets) |
| **Traceability** | actions are recorded in the [audit logs](/en/docs/securite/audit-logs) |
| **Account** | [2FA (TOTP)](/en/docs/compte/securite) and trusted devices |
| **Network** | applications exposed over HTTPS only, automatic TLS certificates |
