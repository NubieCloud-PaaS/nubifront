Everything happens from **Organization → Plan**. Only the **Owner** can act on the plan.

> ℹ️ **Paid plans are not activated in self-service.** You **request** them from the console; our team gets back to you and activates the plan. Moving **back to the Free plan** stays immediate and in your hands.

## Requesting a paid plan

1. Open the **Plan** page: the grid shows the plans, with your current plan highlighted.
2. Choose the **billing region** (Africa / Europe) to see the right prices in FCFA.
3. Select the plan you want and confirm: a **request** is sent to our team.
4. We get back to you about the terms, then activate the plan. **Your quotas increase at that point**, not before.

You can also write to us directly at **contact@nubitech.io**.

> 💡 Nothing is interrupted while the request is pending: your organization keeps running on its current plan.

## Moving back to the Free plan

Select **FREE** and confirm: the change is **immediate**.

If your consumption exceeds the Free plan's quotas, the console **refuses** the change and tells you so — free up resources first (delete deployments or projects), then try again.

## Effect on quotas

- **When a paid plan is activated**: your quotas (CPU / RAM / Storage, number of projects and members) move to that plan's values.
- **When moving back down**: they shrink accordingly. Hence the refusal above when your usage does not fit.

The plan sets the **total** for the organization; that total is then split across your projects — see [Quotas](/en/docs/organisations/quotas).
