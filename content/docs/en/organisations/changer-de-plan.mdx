---
title: Changing plan
description: Moving from one plan to another (upgrade/downgrade) and the effect on quotas.
---

Changing plan is done from **Organization → Plan**. Only the **Owner** can change the plan.

## How

1. Open the **Plan** page: the grid shows the plans (FREE, PROFESSIONAL, BUSINESS, ENTERPRISE), with your current plan highlighted.
2. Choose the **billing region** (Africa / Europe) to see the right prices in FCFA.
3. Select the new plan and confirm.

## Effect on quotas

- **Upgrade**: your quotas (CPU / RAM / Storage, number of projects and members) increase immediately.
- **Downgrade**: quotas shrink. If your current consumption exceeds the new limits, the console warns you — reduce your usage first.

> ℹ️ For the **ENTERPRISE** plan (on request), contact the sales team: it cannot be selected in self-service.
