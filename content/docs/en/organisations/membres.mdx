## Organization roles

| Role | Description |
|------|-------------|
| **Owner** | The creator. Only one per organization. Full rights. |
| **Admin** | Manages members and projects. |
| **Member** | Accesses the projects and resources they are attached to. |

### Permissions

| Action | Owner | Admin | Member |
|--------|:-----:|:-----:|:------:|
| Invite a member | ✅ | ✅ | — |
| Change a member's role | ✅ | — | — |
| Remove a member | ✅ | ✅ | — |
| Change plan | ✅ | — | — |
| Delete the organization | ✅ | — | — |

## Invite a member

1. On **Members**, click **Invite a member**.
2. Enter the **email** and choose the **role** (Member or Admin).
3. The person receives an invitation email.

## Invitations

| Detail | Value |
|--------|--------|
| Validity | **30 days** |
| Recipient **already registered** | accepts and joins the organization directly |
| **New** recipient | creates their account from the invitation, then validates their email |

## Remove a member

If the member **owns resources** (projects, environments, applications), the console first shows a **transfer preview**: those resources will be **transferred to the organization's owner**. Confirm to finish.

> ℹ️ Removing a member does not delete their resources: they change owner (to the Owner).
