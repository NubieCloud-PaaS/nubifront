The **organization** is the top-level container: it carries the **plan**, the resource **quotas** and your team's **members**. You create your [projects](/en/docs/projets) inside it.

## Create an organization

From **Organizations**, click **Create an organization** and give it a **name**. You automatically become its **Owner**.

> ℹ️ **Every organization starts on the Free plan.** Paid plans are activated by NubieCloud once the commercial terms are agreed — see [Changing plan](/en/docs/organisations/changer-de-plan). The Free plan has **no time limit**: there is no rush.

## The plans

| Plan | CPU | RAM | Storage | Projects | Members | How to get it |
|------|----:|----:|--------:|:--------:|:-------:|:--------------|
| **FREE** | 0.5 vCPU | 1 GB | 5 GB | 1 | 2 | immediate, at creation |
| **PROFESSIONAL** | 8 vCPU | 16 GB | 200 GB | 5 | 20 | on request |
| **BUSINESS** | 24 vCPU | 48 GB | 600 GB | unlimited | unlimited | on request |
| **ENTERPRISE** | custom | custom | custom | unlimited | unlimited | on quote |

### Pricing

Prices are in **FCFA per month** and depend on the **billing region**:

| Plan | Africa | Europe |
|------|-------:|-------:|
| FREE | 0 | 0 |
| PROFESSIONAL | 145,000 | 100,000 |
| BUSINESS | 445,000 | 305,000 |
| ENTERPRISE | on request | on request |

> 💡 The **FREE** plan is free of charge and **has no time limit** — ideal for testing. To move to a higher plan, **request it** from the console: our team replies and activates it.

> ℹ️ The plan sets the **total** resources of the organization. That total is then shared out across your projects — see [Quotas](/en/docs/organisations/quotas).
