The organization's **Settings** page gathers the general configuration, the **regions** and deletion.

## General

- Change the organization's **name**.

## Regions

This is where you manage the **deployment regions**: global Nubiecloud regions and **custom regions** (your own infrastructure). You can add one, **connect** it, disconnect it or reactivate it.

See the full guide: [Regions & Connect](/en/docs/regions).

## Delete the organization

Restricted to the **owner**, in the **danger zone**. The journey has several steps:

1. Check of the attached **resources** (projects, environments, applications).
2. Preview of what will be deleted.
3. Confirmation: you type the **exact name** of the organization and tick the confirmation box (a reason can be given).

> ⚠️ Deletion is a **soft delete**: the data is kept for **90 days** before permanent removal. Contact support within that window for a possible restore.
