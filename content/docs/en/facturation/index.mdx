Your **organization plan** determines your quotas (CPU, RAM, Storage, projects, members, NubiS3 buckets) and the associated price.

## The plans and their prices

The full detail of the quotas and prices (in FCFA, per **Africa** / **Europe** region) is on the **[Creating an organization & choosing your plan](/en/docs/organisations)** page.

Monthly prices, as a reminder:

| Plan | Africa | Europe |
|------|-------:|-------:|
| FREE | 0 | 0 |
| PROFESSIONAL | 145,000 FCFA | 100,000 FCFA |
| BUSINESS | 445,000 FCFA | 305,000 FCFA |
| ENTERPRISE | on request | on request |

## Estimating the cost of your resources

When you configure a **project**, the console shows a **cost estimate** based on the resources (CPU / RAM / Storage) you reserve. It is an **order of magnitude** to help you size your projects, not an invoice.

## Getting a paid plan

Every organization starts on the **Free** plan. Paid plans are obtained **on request**: from **Organization → Plan**, the owner selects the plan they want, and our team gets back to them about the terms before activating it. Your quotas increase **on activation**, not on request.

Moving **back to Free** stays immediate and in your hands — refused only if your consumption exceeds the Free plan's quotas.

See [Changing plan](/en/docs/organisations/changer-de-plan).

## Quotas and overrun

When a resource reaches its limit, the creation of new resources is **blocked** until something is freed or the plan is upgraded. See [Understanding quotas](/en/docs/organisations/quotas).

## Payment & invoicing

> ℹ️ **Online payment and invoicing are not available in the console.** That is also why paid plans go through a request: the terms (bank transfer, Mobile Money) and any **Enterprise quote** are settled with our team, at **contact@nubitech.io**.

The console lets you **estimate** the cost of your resources and **request** a plan; it does not take payment.
